Keys

Uses the instrument above. A is Sa and S D F G H J are Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni, up to K for taar Sa; the row above (W E T Y U) gives the komal swars and tivra Ma. Z/X move an octave down/up. A MIDI keyboard plays at its own pitch unless you choose a Sa key for it below, with the sustain pedal.

Sargam practice

Starts on sam

    Brackets share one beat: S R (GM) P (DNS'). [beats] sets a length that carries on until changed: S[0.5] R G M[2]. - holds the previous swar, ~ is a rest. S^R moves to Re without a new attack, D/S' slides up to Sa' (meend).

    While the line editor is open, each swar you play is added at its cursor, with a - for each step you hold it (at the tempo and Laya). While the taal plays (or, if that option is on, with the taal stopped, timed silently at its tempo from the first swar on sam), all you play is written as notation as you go, a line per avartan: faster swars as groups like (SR), holds as -, rests as ~. Click outside the text box to play from the computer keys; there - holds the swar, \ adds a | and Backspace removes the last swar.

    One line = one avartan. Komal swars are underlined, tivra Ma has a mark above, dots show mandra (below) and taar (above) saptak, small raised swars are grace swars. Use headphones when checking your singing.
